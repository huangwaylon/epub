## Characters
**成瀬あかり Naruse Akari**. Kyoto University Faculty of Science first-year (一回生), commutes from Zeze; tourism ambassador, Friend Mart cashier, patroller. Plain, stiff, declarative speech (〜だ, 〜だろう, 〜ではないか, すまない) to all. English: formal, clipped, few contractions, never archaic. Fixed: おう → "Hey."; そうか → "I see."; 構わない → "I don't mind."; いいだろう → "Very well."; 礼には及ばない → "No need to thank me."; ほう (pleased) → "Oh?" Bare surnames for everyone (坪井, 梅谷, 田中, 西浦, 城山, 篠原, 島崎); 大曽根氏/橿原氏 → "Osone-shi," "Kashihara-shi"; 会長 → "the President"; strangers 君 → "you", 貴殿 → "sir". Parents: 父 "my father," 母さん "Mom." Eats slowly (30 chews), bed at nine, always draws 大吉, remembers every name and exam number. Gains a faint smile by the end.

**島崎みゆき Shimazaki Miyuki**. In Tokyo studying English; narrator of ch7–8: wry, fond, a little jealous of Naruse's new circle. 成瀬 bare; Onuki is ぬっきー "Nukki."

**坪井さくら Tsuboi Sakura** (つぼい). From rural Kanagawa, lives alone near Demachiyanagi; flat-faced, shy, bitter at first. Followed 早田くん **Hayata-kun** (はやた) to the wrong university (he went to Todai). Calls her 成瀬さん, switching to bare **成瀬** at the end of ch1 — keep the switch. Umetani calls her 坪井氏; Osone "Sakura-chan." Teachers 松尾先生 Matsuo-sensei, 岡本先生 Okamoto-sensei; adviser 高橋さん Takahashi-san.

**梅谷誠 Umetani Makoto** (うめたにまこと). Agriculture first-year, born in Kitashirakawa; Morimi devotee narrating in mock-grand literary 私 (formal, bookish, faux-classical asides — keep the pomposity; this is the one voice that may sound old-fashioned). Uses -shi for everyone: 成瀬氏, 坪井氏, ののか氏, 大曽根氏, 橿原氏, 西浦氏. His father: "君にはまだ見えないのだな" → "You can't see it yet."

**Daruma Research Society (達磨研究会)**: **木崎輝翔 Kizaki Kirato**, 会長 "the President" (Engineering, 2nd year, Aichi, bearded, kimono, girl-shy, stiff speech like Naruse's); **大曽根隼人 Osone Hayato**, 鍋奉行 "the Hotpot Boss" (Economics, Nagoya, handsome, cooks, "Any food allergies?", calls girls "Akari-chan"; 俺 when angry); **橿原裕典 Kashihara Hironori**, 数式男 "Equation Guy" (Engineering, Nara, Kansai dialect, blunt; mahjong-parlour nametag かっし～ "Kasshi~").

**田中ののか Tanaka Nonoka**. Ritsumeikan 2nd year from Nara; streamer "Bokinonoka"; black dress, twin tails; attention-hungry, excitable, self-dramatizing, internet slang, Kansai slips (なんやねん → "what the heck"). Viewers call her ののたん "Nonotan." She calls everyone by bare surname; Naruse calls her 田中.

**成瀬美貴子 Naruse Mikiko**. Mother; quiet, dry Kansai (そうなんや, せやろな → "Figures."), fiercely loyal. **成瀬慶彦 Naruse Yoshihiko**, father: weepy, proud. **咲子 Sakiko**, Mikiko's blunt younger sister (Hikone). **品川沙弥香 Shinagawa Sayaka**, Biwa TV staffer; director 坂本さん Sakamoto-san.

**西浦航一郎 Nishiura Koichiro**. Now at a Kyoto university; huge, shy, letter-writing, lovesick; 成瀬さん "Naruse-san"; nickname にっしゃん "Nisshan." Friend 結希人 Yukito.

Recurring: 城山 Shiroyama (YouTuber, Engineering, "Shiroyama-kun" to Shimazaki); 篠原かれん Shinohara Karen (bare 篠原/成瀬 with Naruse; "Shinohara-san" to Shimazaki); 北川みらい Kitagawa Mirai ("Mirai-chan"); 呉間言実/祐生 Kurema Kotomi/Yusei ("Koto-chan"); 吉嶺マサル Yoshimine Masaru; 稲枝敬太 Inae Keita; 大貫かえで Onuki Kaede.

## Tone, voice, pitfalls
Book 3 widens Naruse's orbit into a found family that converges at Otsu harbor. Keep the deadpan (Naruse petting every part of the bronze ox), the teasing romance (Nishiura), and the quiet grief and pride (Mikiko, Shimazaki's "I'll live to two hundred too"). Voices must stay distinct: Sakura flat and wounded; Umetani pompous pastiche; Nonoka hyper streamer slang; Mikiko sparse Kansai; Nishiura bashful; Shimazaki wry.
