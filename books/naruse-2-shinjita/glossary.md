## Characters
**成瀬あかり Naruse Akari**. Now 18–19: Zeze High senior, then Kyoto University Faculty of Science first-year (一回生 "first-year"). Plain, stiff, declarative speech (〜だ, 〜だろう, 〜ではないか, すまない) to everyone, including customers and adults, which others remark on. English: formal, clipped, few contractions, never archaic. Fixed: おう → "Hey."; そうか → "I see."; 構わない → "I don't mind."; いいだろう → "Very well."; それはよかった → "Good." Addresses people by bare surname (島崎, 北川, 篠原, 城山); 君 → "you"; Kotomi **呉間氏 "Kurema-shi."** Calls parents 父さん "Dad," 母さん "Mom"; refers to 母 "my mother." Can switch to です/ます on request (ch4: "Yes, I can speak politely if I choose to"). Instagram voice = "Naruse style": 瀬をはやみ～の成瀬あかりでございます → "Se wo hayami~, this is Naruse Akari," followed by stilted keigo; letters signed 成瀬あかり拝 → "Respectfully, Naruse Akari." Remembers every name and face; gets 大吉 every year; asleep by nine.

**島崎みゆき Shimazaki Miyuki**. Naruse's lifelong friend, Otsu High, then Tokyo university (English). Casual, warm, ironic narrator of ch5; insecure about being replaced. Calls Naruse 成瀬; Naruse's parents お父さん/お母さん → in direct address "Mr. Naruse"/"Mrs. Naruse," in narration "Naruse's father/mother."

**成瀬慶彦 Naruse Yoshihiko** (よしひこ). Father, bank-branch employee, soft, anxious, sentimental; keeps a scrapbook of Akari's press clippings; compares her to the racehorse Deep Impact. Calls her あかり; calls Shimazaki みゆきちゃん.
**成瀬美貴子 Naruse Mikiko** (みきこ). Mother; terse, unflappable, dry Kansai ("なにしてくれてんねん" → "What have you done?"). Calls him あなた → "you"; この人 (to others) → "he" / "my husband."

**北川みらい Kitagawa Mirai**. Tokimeki Elementary, grade 4→5; cries easily, earnest, Zezekara superfan, becomes Naruse's patrol "disciple" (弟子). Childlike voice: short sentences, exclamations. Calls them 成瀬さん, 島崎さん; Naruse calls her 北川. Classmates: 野原結芽 Nohara Yume ("Yume-chan"), たいちゃん Tai-chan, くらっち Kuratchi, 璃央ちゃん Rio-chan; teacher 奥村先生 Okumura-sensei.

**城山友樹 Shiroyama Tomoki**. Kochi ronin, YouTuber "Tomo" (541 subscribers), on a "Kyoto University exam for 3,000 yen" challenge; polite (です/ます) to adults, casual with Naruse; passes Engineering. Naruse: 城山; Shimazaki: 城山くん.

**呉間言実 Kurema Kotomi** (くれまことみ). Housewife, compulsive complainer, self-aware and self-mocking; prickly, secretly fond of Naruse (Shimazaki: "tsundere"). Narration crisp, fussy, comic. Husband **呉間祐生 Kurema Yusei** (ゆうせい): endlessly easygoing, calls her ことちゃん "Koto-chan." Father-in-law **呉間一雄 Kurema Kazuo** (Kobe), legendary complainer: 祝ってへんのか → "So you're not celebrating?" Store staff: 大澤幸雄 Osawa Yukio (manager), 園田明慶 Sonoda Akiyoshi (deputy), 有村 Arimura; shoplifter 佐々木さん Sasaki-san → narration 万引きババア "the shoplifting hag."

**篠原かれん Shinohara Karen**. 19, second-year, from Sakamoto; granddaughter/daughter of former ambassadors, father a city councillor. Bright, image-conscious, secretly a rail photographer (撮り鉄) with an alt account (裏垢); youthful casual voice, internet slang, occasional blunt "うるせえ!" → "Shut up!" Naruse and Karen use bare surnames: 篠原 / 成瀬. Family: 裕章 Hiroaki (father), ゆりえ Yurie (mother), 景子 Keiko (maternal grandmother), 春江 Harue (paternal grandmother), 裕介 Yusuke (brother). Association staff 藤野さん Fujino-san, 米田さん Yoneda-san; candidate 阿部 Abe.

Recurring from book 1: 吉嶺マサル Yoshimine Masaru (festival chair), 稲枝 Inae; オーロラソース Aurora Sauce; マヨネーズ隅田 Mayonnaise Sumida; Nishiura (the unnamed "friend in Hiroshima," ch2).

## Tone, voice, pitfalls
Each chapter is an outsider slowly realizing Naruse is on their side. Humor is deadpan and situational; sentiment is understated (father's fear of the empty house, Mirai's tears, Shimazaki's jealousy). Keep narrators distinct: child plainness (ch1), fond dad bathos (ch2), Kotomi's pedantic self-mockery (ch3), Karen's breezy slang and hidden hurt (ch4–5), Shimazaki's wry warmth (ch6–7). Kansai lines (Mikiko, strangers, grannies) → colloquial English, no fake accent.

Drafts exist only for ch1, 4–7 (none for ch2–3). Systematic errors found in spot-checks:
1. **Misalignment** (common): ch1 #302 「わたしでよければ話聞くけど」 has the draft of a later unit ("Shimazaki-san let out a reluctant 'Ehhh?'…"); ch7 #349, #416, #418 shifted by one or more units; ch7 #482 merges two units.
2. **Punctuation**: 「」 kept in English (ch1, ch4 throughout); dialogue without quotes.
3. **Honorific drift**: 篠原さん → "Ms. Shinohara," 吉嶺マサルさん → "Mr. Yoshimine Masaru"; use "Shinohara-san," "Yoshimine-san."
4. **Western name order**: "Akari Naruse" in the Jan 1 message; use "Naruse Akari."
5. **Register**: Naruse softened into chatty English or inflated into archaic; small meaning drifts ("that would lose Akari's trust" for あかりの信用を失う → "we'd lose Akari's trust").
