# Thursday Cocoa (木曜日にはココアを), Aoyama Michiko — translator's glossary

Follow docs/translation.md. Book title: *Thursday Cocoa*. "Ch. N" = story number (p-002 = ch. 1 … p-013 = ch. 12); units are per file. Twelve linked stories; each narrator appears as a minor figure in a neighbour's story. Don't explain links the text only hints at (don't name the Master in ch. 2, the waiter in ch. 4, Mary in ch. 10).

## 2. Characters

Hepburn, no macrons. Sydney chapters: dialogue between Australians, and Mary/Mako/Cindy/Grace/Mark, is English rendered in Japanese; translate as natural English, no -san, no Japanese-style address.

- **ワタル Wataru** — cafe manager, 23. 僕. Polite, earnest, shy. Master/Misako: "Wataru-kun". Ch. 4 "the waiter", ch. 12 "you".
- **ココアさん Cocoa-san** — the fixed nickname, used both ways: Wataru's for Mako (ch. 1), Mako's for Wataru (ch. 12). Keep it identical so the mirror lands.
- **マスター Master** — owner of Marble Cafe, Kyoto gallery owner, all-round fixer; ~50, small, mole in the middle of his forehead. 俺; drawling, casual, ends with ね/の. Capitalized, no article when used as a name ("Call me Master"). Ch. 10 unit 98–99 plays on MASTER's meanings: keep "Master", list the senses in English.
- **朝美 Asami** (ch. 2) — 私. Brisk, competitive, self-mocking. **輝也 Teruya** — husband, painter of trick art, soft 僕, gentle authority. **拓海 Takumi / たっくん Takkun** — 5, mushroom haircut, plays airplane. Calls parents おかあさん/おとうさん → "Mommy/Daddy". Adults' ママ/パパ → "Mommy/Daddy". 輝也パパ → "Daddy Teruya".
- **添島瑠々 Soejima Ruru** — child; her mother is **ボーダーさん "Stripes-san"** (Asami's private name; ボーダー添島さん "Stripes Soejima-san").
- **えな先生 Ena-sensei** (ch. 3 narrator) — 20-ish kindergarten teacher; Asami miscalls her "Eri-sensei".
- **萌香 Moeka-chan**; her mother.
- **泰子 Yasuko** — Yasuko-sensei at work; to Risa **やっちゃん Yatchan**. 36, blunt, bossy (あなたねぇ, ～しなさい; to Risa あんた "you"). Keep her harshness; it hides care.
- **理沙 Risa** — Yasuko's high-school friend, temp worker, timid, then fiercely earnest. Husband **ひろゆき Hiroyuki** (hiragana only) → "Hiroyuki-san" in her voice.
- **美佐子 Misako** — 72, gentle old-fashioned feminine speech (かしら, ～ですのよ, ましたよ); period-polite English, not caricature. **進一郎 Shinichiro** — her husband, mild, plain speech. They call each other "Shinichiro-san", "Misako-san". **陽介 Yosuke** — her ex. **尋子 Hiroko / ピー Pi / ピーちゃん Pi-chan** — their daughter, 36, lingerie designer in Tokyo (MARIA).
- **優 Yu** (ch. 7) — 私; signs paintings "You" — the English pun ("like being called 'you'") works directly.
- **ラルフ Ralph** — nearly 40, Aussie, corny jokes, "G'day!" with a wink. Storyteller's ラルフさん → "Ralph" (the です/ます fairy-tale tone carries the warmth). Cindy's またね、オレンジさん → "See you, Mr. Orange."
- **シンディ Cindy** — aromatherapist-witch, 15 years younger than Ralph.
- **グレイス Grace** — botanist, aroma teacher, "talks with flowers." Cindy's グレイス先生 → "Miss Grace" (address 先生 → "Miss"); Atsuko → "Grace".
- **アツコ Atsuko / あっちゃん Atchan** (Pi's childhood friend) — 36, わたし, reflective, wry. **マーク Mark** — Aussie interior designer, "No worries!", dogs love him. Dog **ジャック Jack**.
- **マコ Mako** — Ena's older cousin, English-school teacher, writes the MACO column in CANVAS; chestnut hair, fountain pen, tricolor airmail. Ena: "Mako-chan".
- **メアリー Mary** — Mako's host sister, five years older, weak heart; in her past-life vision Mako is お姉ちゃん "big sister".

## 3. Places, objects, motifs

- マーブル・カフェ **Marble Cafe** (ASCII). By the river at the end of the 桜並木 **row of cherry trees**; the 橋 bridge leads to the kindergarten, Pi's shop, the station.
- いつもの席 / 窓際、隅の席 "her usual seat" / "the corner seat by the window".
- ココア **cocoa**; ホットココア "hot cocoa". Fixed lines:
  - 「ホットココアを、お願いします」 "A hot cocoa, please."
  - 「ホットココアでございます。お熱いので、お気をつけください」 "Here is your hot cocoa. It's hot, so please be careful." Ch. 12 unit 68 repeats the second sentence verbatim; ch. 12 unit 20's 「熱いから気をつけてね」 must contrast: "Careful, it's hot, okay?"
  - 「ホットココアでお温まりください」 "Warm yourself up with a hot cocoa."
  - 「好きなところ／場所にいるだけで、元気になることもある（と思います）」 "Sometimes just being somewhere you love can make you feel better." (ch. 1 66, ch. 11 42, ch. 12 42–45)
  - 「見る目だけはあるんだ」 "I've got an eye for people, if nothing else." (ch. 1, ch. 10)
  - ココアのハート "the cocoa heart"; 「My dear best friend, Mary」 as is.
- Title colors as given; "Grey" British spelling.
- エアメール "airmail letter"; トリコロールの封筒 "tricolor-bordered envelope"; 便箋 "letter paper".
- 桜 / サクラ: kanji → "cherry blossoms/cherry trees"; Mary's katakana サクラ (a word she learned) → "sakura". ジャカランダ "jacaranda".
- 秋の桜 "autumn cherry blossoms" (ch. 6, a wish on the cafe's Christmas tree). The riddle rests on 秋桜 = cosmos; translate what's said, no gloss. (For translators: Sydney's April is autumn, Mary's visit.)
- 卵焼き "tamagoyaki"; 弁当 "bento"; 菜の花畑 "a field of rapeseed blossoms".
- サムシング・フォー "the four somethings" (something old/new/borrowed/blue); サムシング・ブルー "something blue". ショーツ『ＭＡＲＩＡ』 "shorts called 'MARIA'"; マリアブルー "Maria blue".
- 赤い糸／運命の赤い糸 "the red string of fate". ロマンスグレー "distinguished grey / silver-haired" (never "romance grey").
- ロリキート lorikeet; ベジマイト Vegemite; Shinichiro's misreading ベゲミテ → "Veg-eh-mee-teh?"
- ちちんぷいぷい "chichin puipui" (CHICHIN PUIPUI as printed); mizuiro kept; ターコイズ turquoise.
- 『ＣＡＮＶＡＳ』 CANVAS (Sydney free paper); ＭＡＣＯ column; ワーキングホリデー "working holiday"; ギリホリ "a just-under-the-age-limit working holiday".
- Sydney: Taronga Zoo, Circular Quay, Harbour Bridge, Opera House, Royal Botanic Garden / "the Botanic Garden", Paddington, Ralph's Kitchen ("the orange shop"), グッダイ "G'day!".
- Embedded English with a Japanese gloss (「I can talk with flowers.（私はお花と話せます）」, 「No worries!（問題ないよ！）」): give the English once, drop the gloss.

## 4. Tone notes and pitfalls

Tone: gentle, warm, lightly comic; each voice distinct (Wataru's shy wonder, Asami's executive brusqueness, Yasuko's scolding, Misako's chatty grace, the ch. 8 bedtime-story voice). Many one-line paragraphs; keep them short. Onomatopoeia (ぱぱぱっと, ふにゃり) → an English verb or adverb, never romanized. Don't sweeten endings or explain coincidences.

Draft spot-check (~45 units across all chapters). Systematic errors:

1. **Misalignment, merging, and pulling from neighbours.** Ch. 5 units 75–92 are shifted: 75 contains 76–77; 79's draft is "The man answered leisurely." (unit 80); 92's is unit 96. Ch. 4 unit 70 adds "I muttered in a small voice" from 71. Re-translate every unit from its own `ja`.
2. **Padding and paraphrase.** Ch. 1 unit 26 「ホットね」 → "One hot coffee, coming right up." (just "Hot, huh."); 29 expands the Master's line into "to be the Master of my own little café, brewing coffee for my customers"; 44 adds "I've had my fill of this one."
3. **Outright mistranslation.** Ch. 4 unit 90 打撲ですんだ → "it was just a mistake" (it's "only a bruise"). Ch. 11 unit 7 「メアリーにも見せたいわ！」 → "I want to show them to Mary too!" (Mako is speaking to Mary: "I want to show you, Mary!").
4. **Inconsistent names and honorifics.** "Ms. Eri" (should be "Eri-sensei"), "Tak-kun" vs "Takkun", "Teacher, I have a question" (Cindy to Grace: "Miss Grace"), "the Something Four".
5. **Calques that read as Japanese.** Ch. 6 unit 26 "romance-grey hair"; ch. 10 unit 22 "Atsuko's dream will surely come true" (Grace speaking *to* Atsuko: "Your dream will come true, Atsuko"); ch. 10 unit 106 "you really are loved by dogs". Ch. 6 unit 17 renders ベゲミテ as "Vegemite?", losing the misreading.
